# Phase 2: analyze

**Needs:** S. **Produces:** the communication problem (objective, message, weakest link, information groups with reading tiers, tensions), the context profile, the differentiator, the source inventory, a feasibility verdict, and tagged implications. S is not restated. **Guided:** no stop; findings lead the direction entry.
**Order:** triage research → subject (A) → context and viewing (B) → problem (C) → feasibility (D) → differentiator and inventory (E) → implications → gate. A finding that changes no decision is dropped.

## A. The subject as it is
Record values, personality, and positioning shown by what the subject does and says; credibility actually held; the commissioner's aspirations; the audience relationship. Separate fact from interpretation and aspiration from reality; downgrade readings richer than their evidence. Record traits to express and claims not to make. Facts, interpretations, and assumptions stay apart: ChatGPT may interpret aesthetics but must not turn a small fact into an invented identity.

## B. Context and viewing
One profile per distinct context; images sharing a context share it. Start from the Phase 1 inference and test it. Derive from how the image is made, shown, and reached, never from the medium's name.
- **Encounter:** what surrounds it (neighbors' color, density, scale, order, finish; competing stimuli), the audience's state (goal, motion, attention, time, distance, light, culture), the reading path.
- **Reading tiers** (derived, not assumed to be three): **glance** (what a passing viewer must take in at the farthest distance), **approach**, **close**. Tiers are assigned by when the viewer needs an item, never by whether it fits. Glance holds the hero and at most two short items in the order the message needs them; the name takes a slot only where recognition drives the action (a known brand, a brand-led purpose), otherwise the offer or occasion comes first; one line records the choice. Everything else is approach or close. The reading size that suits a distance is about distance ÷ 150 for sustained lines and ÷ 115 for a passing glance (cap height, tagged assumption): used only to judge feasibility, never sent to ChatGPT as a size. **Core content** (what the action depends on: offers, prices, choices) always keeps comfortable reading at its tier; only **reference content** (contacts, hours, fine print) may read deliberately.
- **Medium reading:** what the medium imposes, what the audience relies on, what it affords; record only what changes a decision. Keep a convention the audience relies on where departing costs effort or credibility.
- **Impression vs expression:** hold the impression (what the viewer should feel and conclude) constant; let the setting decide the expression.
- **Departure and fit:** find where neighbors are uniform (color field, density, scale, order, finish, form) and choose where this piece departs (relative, never just louder); record the fit dimensions (conventions relied on, credibility cues). The occasion and culture the brief names are fit dimensions: depart from their apparent signifiers by specificity, never by omitting them.
- If the context cannot be established, state the most probable one and mark it provisional.
- **Audience encounter:** references, motivations, trusted register, trust cues present or lacking, what they must conclude per tier. Drop stereotypes and unsupported psychology.

## C. The communication problem
- **Objective:** a user-stated priority fixes the primary objective; the weakest link is chosen within it and never replaces it. Record the desired response, the necessary information, the desired perception (qualities included).
- **Information groups:** by the job each does toward the objective (carry the proposition, support what is seen first, enable the offer and action, substantiate trust, help evaluation, give practical information), never by similar looks or the user's list order. Per group: items, tier, what it refers to, internal hierarchy, volume. Items that label, price, or explain a depicted thing belong with it.
- **Tensions:** pairs of needs competing for one resource (glance attention, space, legibility, register); state both needs and the resource. Where the conflict priority (`SKILL.md`) or the user's priority decides, record the lead side; Phase 3 decides the rest.
- **Weakest link:** attention → comprehension → credibility → action, those the purpose needs: the one most likely to fail here. Test the brief's own assumptions (images asked, audience assumed, success measure); one that undermines the objective becomes a tension.
- **Message:** one sentence: what the glance viewer takes away and does next. It serves the objective and contradicts no quality.

## D. Feasibility (one pass, only for content-heavy images)
ChatGPT chooses the layout, so the skill checks one thing: can this content be read at this distance in this format? Estimate once, for the most compact sensible arrangement (a hero beside its own text and equal units in columns share one band; extent = the larger): each group's need = planned lines × pitch (≈ 1.6 × cap for display lines, ≈ 2 × cap for body text) at its tier's reading size, as a share of the long side, plus ≈ 15% for gaps; add the hero's minimum extent and an overhead of 24% (structure and decoration 5, quiet 8, margins and separations 8, headroom 3). Over 100% is **dense**.
Dense is answered by structure, never by deleting mandatory content: (1) group and tier the content so core items lead and reference items recede; (2) simplify the hero before shrinking core content; (3) tell the user once the way out (larger format, less text, a second image); (4) put the tiers and a density note into the brief (`4_brief_composition.md`). A dense verdict never becomes numbers in the brief.

## E. Differentiator and visual source inventory
- **Differentiation:** list candidate differentiators from specifics, process, materials, origin, behavior, offer logic, location, people, name, ritual, and what each peer already claims. Test each: **true** (failure discards), **relevant** at the encounter, **distinctive**, **visualizable** (a failure may still shape tone or hierarchy). Choose a strategy; if none is strong, the piece differs through idea and execution, and say so. The choice must fail the **swap test**: a named peer substituted makes it false.
- **Culture:** identify the cultures, languages, and conventions actually relevant (a national label names many aesthetics); preserve the user's register; use regional or traditional references only where the subject truly connects.
- **Inventory** (the raw material for specificity; it feeds direction and is never pasted into the brief as a list): mine practices and rituals; materials and tools with their surface and ornament traditions; how things are made, handled, shared; daily spatial and sensory detail; language and lettering traditions; geography and seasons; the subject's history. Screen each specific: **true**, **respectful** (no tokenism), **meaning verified** (omit a loaded symbol if uncertain), **workable** (can be seen and depicted truthfully). Record (1) the apparent baseline, (2) the deeper true specifics (at least one beyond the baseline), (3) the workable ones.

## F. Research
A question, not a phase: run only for a question that names a direction decision it could change and that the brief and knowledge cannot answer. At most three targeted queries and one page read per question; no trend surveys; none needed means record "no research needed". Prefer first-hand sources; competitor visuals are a baseline to differ from, never a model. Without tools, reason from the brief and mark conclusions provisional.

## Implications and gate
Write each finding as "because [finding], the brief must / should / must avoid [consequence]", tagged given, researched, or inferred.
**Gate (fails if any is false):** every finding changes or confirms a named decision · differentiation passes the swap test (or is "fixed by evidence") · the profile names its departure and fit dimensions · the objective follows the user's priority · the inventory holds at least one non-emblem specific, every symbol verified, no competitor as source · the message is one sentence serving the objective · groups follow job, tier, and referent · glance slots are justified · a dense verdict, if any, has its way out. Then append the record.
