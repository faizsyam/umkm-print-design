# Phase 3: direction

**Needs:** S; Phase 2 output. **Produces:** the **direction record**: core idea, style (lineage and defining traits), hero, color stance, type voice, hierarchy and grouping relations, supporting-visual stance, boundaries, identity or figure decisions where a new one is wanted; the runner-up and decisive criterion; tension carries; obligations. **Guided:** show the cards (§5) and stop for the choice.
**Order:** idea → style → hero → the rest (each reads the one before; no style card before the idea is chosen) → direction check → gate.

## 1. What is decided here, and what is not
Decided: the idea; the lineage and traits of the look; what the hero truly is; color and type as **stance** (roles and character); the relations that carry the content (what leads, what groups with what, where quiet stays); boundaries. Left to ChatGPT: placement, sizes, spacing, font names, lighting setup, the position and count of decorative or supporting elements, and the wording of its own prompt. A direction states intent and a few defining traits, never a specification. Where a decision is Fixed by the user, record it and move on.

## 2. Exploration protocol (per Open or Steered decision)
Fixed: no cards. Steered: run on the open remainder, at least 2 cards. Open: at least 3. Content volume and crowding never raise openness; they raise feasibility work.
1. **Name the apparent option**: what a generic brief for this category and place produces first, the benchmark to beat and never a default. State the pressure the decision resolves (a requirement, a tension, the weakest link). Derive cards from the pressure, not the category.
2. **Write the cards**: at most one apparent, at least one from beyond it. Cards differ on a **structural axis** (what carries identity, what the viewer does first, how information is structured, register, image-making method); color or ornament alone do not count. Sources differ too: product truth, audience moment, environment, differentiator, name or origin, material, the inventory. Fill every field in specialist terms (a label is not a card).
3. **Judge** the last-written first. Eliminate any card contradicting a quality or mandatory content (unless the conflict priority forces the trade). Compare survivors only where they differ, on lenses that discriminate: qualities; context (neighbors, departure and fit); whether the card can carry the content hierarchy (feasibility); truth and culture; distinctiveness.
4. **Challenge once:** name the runner-up and the strongest case against the leader. The leader's margin is one decisive criterion naming an observable property. If none can be stated after step 5's rebuild, the cards tie: take the simpler one and record the tie. An apparent option wins only by naming the criterion the others failed.
5. **Test the leader.** *Predictable:* could a stranger guess it from category and place? Rebuild once; a second failure takes the best resolution, recorded (a convention the audience relies on stays). *Specific:* nameable by lineage and traits, failing unchanged for a competitor; strike category and medium words (flat, vector, sticker, cartoon, pop) and bare mood adjectives (cute, modern, clean) from the look: what remains must still describe a drawable look. *Ambitious:* a merely competent card beats an ambitious one only on a stated criterion.
6. **Engineer:** each material weakness gets a named move or an accepted cost; each key strength a place it is amplified. Select, transplant a strength, repair, or combine as a **hybrid** (candidates under one logic, statable as one idea); a compromise needing "and also" is rejected.
7. **Record:** chosen, runner-up, decisive criterion, kept strengths; obligations a later phase must honor.
**Specialist terms:** use a term only if definable. Style = lineage and defining traits; imagery = depiction mode, linework, technique, surface, light, finish; color = hue family, value structure, chroma, temperature, area proportion; type = voice, classification, stroke contrast, proportion, spacing; composition = axis, grouping logic, figure-ground, balance, layering.

## 3. The parts of a direction
**Card fields:** name · the idea in one sentence (a true thing about the subject joined to something the audience recognizes; a mood word is not an idea) · the look in 4 to 6 defining traits · the hero · how it carries the hierarchy (what leads, how groups sit, where space stays open) · main risk and its move · fit with each quality.
- **Core idea:** specific, visual, generative (the rest follows from it), truthful, functional (reads at a glance without decoding).
- **Style:** one synthesis in specialist terms: lineage and borrowed traits (never a living artist, studio, or protected character), how forms and lines are built, surface and finish, light and chromatic stance, lettering voice. Renderability is judged on what must be exact (text, counted forms, mark geometry), never as a reason to pick the plainest look: a concretely described rich style usually renders well.
- **Hero:** what it is down to true specifics; chosen only when one subject deserves primary attention and can be depicted truthfully (where the viewer must see exactly what is sold, a mode that stylizes it away is ineligible); otherwise no hero or a small equal set. Its scale and edge express the message. A figure (mascot, character) is decided at identity level: kind, character, three to five distinctive features, proportions, consistency across images; pose and rendering stay open unless decided. A supplied figure is never redesigned. Never a real likeness.
- **Color stance:** roles and rough proportions (a calm dominant field, a supporting field, a sparing accent, an ink for text), harmony, temperature, and value key (light or dark balance by area, ground included): the key must support every quality, since dark masses make a low-key piece however light the accents. Colors the user named are fixed. Text color is chosen for legibility. Hex only for supplied brand colors.
- **Type voice:** what the lettering says about this subject, the roles that must differ (display, informational, numerals), their traits. No font names unless a brand requires one. Letters are never compressed, stretched, or slanted to fit.
- **Hierarchy and grouping** (the one composition decision worth making beforehand): exactly one first read at glance, then the order; groups formed by job and referent; what belongs with the hero; where quiet stays open and what it separates; the balance stance and its counterweight; the reading route. Relations only, never placement. When content is dense, relief comes from structure and grouping, not ornament.
- **Supporting visuals and density:** every element carries a purpose (function, what is lost without it, its effect). Enrich before adding: deepen the hero and lettering, then link major elements, then decided atmosphere, then small supporters. Resources are counted by kind, never by theme; defaults by load class: dense up to 2 supporter families, 3 container designs, 3 rendering-treatment kinds (outline, shadow, gradient, texture, highlight, dimension); moderate 4, 4, 3; light 6, 5, 4. Exceed only by naming the deficit that earns it and the family dropped to pay for it. Plainness is a decision, not a default.
- **Identity (new mark or wordmark):** a supplied one is exact. A new one is decided as an **idea**: an ownable, grounded device that ties the name to the subject (never a styled name alone), weighed against one alternative; construction is left to ChatGPT. The name's letters stay complete and in order. If the hero or idea already fixes the form, only the device is open.
- **Boundaries:** what the piece must never become, from apparent options, neighbors' look, and model defaults; each later stated as a positive opposite.
- **Point of tension:** at most one deliberate break from the piece's own system, only where it lowers reading effort or raises delight; none when the idea already supplies the unexpected.
- **Tensions:** confirm each lead side against the assembled direction and record what the other side keeps.

## 4. Direction check (once)
Do idea, style, hero, color, type, and relations reinforce each other, and do accepted costs compound? Does the direction answer the weakest link in the real setting and hold with the name removed? Does every quality have a visible carrier, and does any decision signal its opposite? Can the content hierarchy be carried at the stated distance? Does anything here prescribe what ChatGPT would decide as well (the necessity test, `4_brief_composition.md`)? Conflicts return to the responsible decision; two cycles at most.

## 5. Guided entry (cards)
Show only the choice, its reason, and the alternatives, in the user's language, skimmable in seconds: no internal terms for a new user.
```
**Step 3 of 4: creative direction**
What I learned: [one or two lines: biggest challenge, surroundings, how it can stand out]
| Direction | What it looks like | Why / trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **[name]**: [idea, ≤ 12 words] | [2 or 3 traits] | [reason tied to the subject] · [weakness] |
| [name] | ... | ... |
You said "…" → carried as: …
OK, or tell me what to change?
```
2 or 3 rows; the chosen one first; matters already fixed by the user go in one line "Already settled: …". Feedback: acknowledge in one line, rerun what it touches, show only changed rows as Before | After, ask once; after two rounds without agreement choose the best resolution, say so, and continue. A question is answered in one or two plain sentences, then the confirmation line repeats.

## Gate (fails if any is false)
Every Open or Steered decision shows its apparent option, runner-up, and decisive criterion · no direction derives from a competitor · the leader passed the three tests and every weakness has a move or accepted cost · the idea fits one sentence and has the five properties · the style passes the strike test · the hero depicts only what is sold or true · color's value key supports every quality · exactly one first read · supporting resources are within the caps and counted by kind · no look-shaping decision is left to chance without being marked open on purpose · every tension is carried. Then append the record.
