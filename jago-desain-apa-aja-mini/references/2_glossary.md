# Glossary: one meaning per term (all phases)

**Read:** once, at start. A term is used only with the meaning below.

## Records and inputs
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Brief Ledger | Phase 1 record of everything known; each item has necessity, provenance, referent, and (text) verbatim form |
| Necessity | **mandatory** (user said it must appear, or the piece cannot work without it; when unsure, mandatory), **optional**, **excluded** |
| Provenance | **given**, **fixed asset**, **inferred**, **researched**, **creative choice** |
| Fixed asset | User-supplied element used exactly as given: logo, brand colors, typefaces, photos, QR/QRIS/codes, mascots, established identity |
| Gap classes | **explicit**, **inferable**, **researchable**, **materially uncertain**. A **blocking gap** is a materially uncertain gap that passes the clarification test and decides one of the four items in `1_intake_and_brief.md` (Gaps) |
| Subject / Brand | What a deliverable represents (business, product, event, person, topic) / the subject's identity as perceived |
| Deliverable | One image the request needs, with its own purpose, medium, format and ratio. Each gets its own composition (Phase 5) and standalone prompt (Phase 6) |
| Design Log | Persisted state: standing block **S** and decisions **D** (`3_standards_and_protocol.md`, State) |
| Carry-forward obligation | A mitigation, accepted cost, or "amplify here" move a later phase must realize or record as accepted |

## Qualities, context, analysis
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Quality requirement | Any experiential, tonal, affective, or priority statement, and any named style, color, or technique, however vague. Binding, not a preference |
| Quality translation | Per quality: visual targets, 2 to 4 **quality carriers** (visible features that deliver it), and contradicting signals |
| Value key | Area-weighted balance of light, mid, dark masses (ground and large bands included), with chroma and temperature: high-, mid-, low-key. Read by area, not accent |
| Communication Problem | The piece's job: objectives, desired response, necessary information (as **information groups**), desired perception, the **message** (the one sentence a glance viewer takes away), the weakest link (attention → comprehension → credibility → action), and tensions |
| Information group | Items that together do one job toward the objective, derived from this brief and never from a template. Records stage, referents, internal hierarchy (members need not share prominence), volume |
| Environment Profile | One per distinct context: the encounter (setting, neighbors, competing stimuli, audience state, culture, reading path), the encounter stages with role floors, the medium reading, and the departure/fit dimensions |
| Medium reading | What the medium **imposes** (what making/displaying does to the image), what the audience **relies on** (learned conventions), what it **affords**. Derived per deliverable; no convention is applied by default |
| Encounter stage | An ordered moment of engagement, derived from medium, environment, audience, purpose: what can be perceived, attention and time, decision, reach (distance, scale, zoom). **Glance** is first (shortest exposure, widest reach), then **approach**, **close**; number is derived. A stage the audience cannot reach cannot carry content. **Stage assignment** gives each item its one stage; items sharing a stage are a **tier**. An item whose parts do different jobs is split at its **glance part** (distinctive name word, price figure, one claim word): only that takes the glance floor; the remainder takes its own stage's floor and stays set with it as a lockup of two sizes |
| Legibility limit / comfortable readability / role floor | **Limit:** smallest size a still, attentive viewer with good contrast can decode, cap height ≈ distance ÷ 345 (assumption); dim light, glare, ornate or condensed forms raise it. **Comfortable readability:** read without effort in real conditions: passing glance ≈ distance ÷ 115, a few seconds or sustained lines ≈ distance ÷ 150. **Role floor:** text taken in at once, in passing, or in long runs sits at comfortable readability; text read deliberately by someone who chose to read it (**reference content**: contacts, addresses, hours, fine print) sits at about 1.5 × the limit in good contrast. **Core content** (what the objective's action depends on: offers, choices, prices, call to action) never takes the deliberate-reading floor. The planning floor is the larger of the role floor and the raster floor, in mm and as % of format height. Prominence is no part of a floor. Computed once in Phase 2; a floor is a minimum, not a target |
| Departure / fit | **Departure:** dimensions where neighbors are uniform and the piece differs to be seen (color field, density, scale, form language, finish, tone). **Fit:** dimensions where it must conform for credibility |
| Audience encounter | What the audience does, believes, and must conclude at each stage (references, motivations, trusted register, trust cues) |
| Visual source inventory | (1) apparent associations as baseline, (2) deeper true specifics, (3) which are visually workable |
| Design implication | "Because [finding], the design must / should / must avoid [consequence]", provenance-tagged |

## Decisions and exploration
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Apparent option | What most people (and the image model) produce first from category, culture, place, or product: a benchmark to beat, never a default. The model's own is the **apparent value**: what it supplies for any undecided attribute |
| Look-shaping attribute | Any visible property that changes what is drawn: composition axis and symmetry, container form, corner/edge geometry, fill mode, plane order, rendering finish and treatments, light, ground, color proportions, lettering and its proportions, how the subject is depicted. One no decision names takes the apparent value |
| Candidate / card | One complete, distinct alternative with the phase's fields filled in specialist terms (a label alone is not a candidate) |
| Structural axis | What candidates must differ on to be distinct: what carries identity, what the viewer does first, how information is structured, register, image-making method. Color or ornament alone do not count |
| Hybrid / compromise | **Hybrid:** candidates combined under one logic, statable as one idea. **Compromise:** needs "and also"; rejected |

## Reliability and risk
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Renderability | Whether the model can draw a thing accurately and repeatably. Falls when: exact data (characters, digits, codes, identities) must reproduce; many independent details must be right; detail is fine relative to resolution; the subject is rare or intricate; it must stay identical across generations; it must survive distortion or overlap. Reduce by design first (fewer, larger, simpler, structured). Never imitated: what depends on external truth (scannable marks, unattachable supplied files, real likenesses, authority marks) |
| Render scale / raster floor | Physical media: output px on the long side ÷ physical long side. Assume ≈ 2,000 px unless stated (tagged). Screen media: displayed reduction turns the smallest readable font (≈ 11 to 12 px sustained, 9 to 10 px brief) into the needed image size. **Raster floor:** exact text is reliable at cap height ≥ ≈ 14 px and garbles below ≈ 10 px. Sizes are finally stated as shares of the format |
| Rendering risk / Volume risk | **Rendering risk:** mandatory text or form in the at-risk band that design cannot reduce further; mitigate by design, then tell the user once what to check. **Volume risk:** mandatory content the fit ladder could not fit at its floors; kept in full and reported once with the way out (larger format, less text, second deliverable) |

## Design system terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Equivalent items | Same content role, rank, and function (list entries, parallel claims, prices): one typographic treatment; size encodes role and rank, never length; layout absorbs length. Repeated containers (rows, cards) also share outer dimensions and internal structure |
| Content role | The function an item serves for the audience and its success measure; fixes treatment grade and reading-order place |
| Purpose line | Per element: **Function** (what it does toward the objective, message, or weakest link, at a stage), **Loss** (what is lost without it; if nothing, remove or merge), **Effect** (what the viewer perceives or does, observable). Two elements with one function merge. |
| Treatment grade | **Designed** (built as a graphic: scale play, emphasis, rhythm, container, motif) or **set** (informational typesetting with role's case, spacing, weight, color, alignment decided) |
| Text-only baseline | The piece with only required text, identity unit, and plain ground: the reference for finding deficits |
| Supporter family | Non-text visual resource: icons; subject/in-use/place imagery; figures; spot illustration; image treatment; pattern/texture; border/frame; divider; pointer; shape block/band/tag; lettering-as-graphic. A tag never imitates a regulated or trust mark |
| Referent / coupling mode | What an item is about / **integrated** (on, around, pointing into the thing), **attached** (same group, shared field/axis/rail), **separate** (own place) |
| Hero group | Hero plus coupled content and supporters, read as one object at glance; its text is subordinate |
| Rank / lever | Place in the attention order at a stage / the means that create it: size, area, position, color, value contrast, weight, container, isolation, plane/overlap, detail density, alignment, type style, edge contact |
| Character carrier / link | A quiet ownable feature that keeps a piece specific at its density / a stated tie between two major elements |
| Visual weight / balance | **Mass class** 1 light, 2 medium, 3 heavy (area and intensity, +1 for isolation or leverage). **Balance:** mass classes summed by side stand in the relation the balance type calls for; read off the region table, never measured. A **counterweight** is a quiet carrier that gains weight from area and distance |
| Weight ceiling | Most weight an element may take; below the information it serves except hero, identity unit (per mark role), and a designed hook |
| Regulated or trust mark | Any mark a viewer takes as proof (authorization, certification, endorsement, rating). Only as supplied; a decorative badge form is fine, one built to read as an official seal is not |
| Container / shape language / form | **Container:** anything that holds, separates, flags, or frames content. **Shape language:** family likeness of all containers, rules, supporters (geometry, corners, edges, line behavior, material impression), from style and subject. **Form:** the container type per function within that language. Weight (fill, edge, padding, shadow) follows content rank |
| Letterform proportions | A role's width class, posture, baseline; fitting never alters them |
| Identity terms | **Wordmark** (name in lettering), **logo mark** (form that can stand for the brand), **lockup**, **identity unit** (lockup as one designed object). **Styled name:** name in an attractive face only: not an identity. **Personality device:** ownable, grounded feature without which the identifier collapses to lettering. **Form class:** the kind of construction a shape belongs to. **Mark role:** unit **leads**, **anchors**, or **signs off**. **Figure:** mascot/character/person, supplied or wanted only; one role per appearance, one design. **Identity forms:** full unit, symbol-only (only where linkable to the name), supplied name-only wordmark |

## Space terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Space intent / free ground | Phase 3 envelope (a table of single numbers summing to 100). **Free ground** = no information, two kinds: **quiet** (contiguous, calm, undecorated, large enough to read as a shape, with a job: separate, isolate, rest, signal positioning) and **atmosphere** (decorated ground carrying the world; occupied, never counted as quiet). Leftover slivers are neither |
| Space plan / space need | Phase 5 quantification of the chosen composition in shares of the format, with axis ledger / a group's load-table extent plus the breathing room and open ground it needs, before any region is drawn |
| Plane / layering | Front, middle, ground (one is allowed). Layer cues: overlap, relative scale, contrast and detail, tonal/temperature step, focus, light, texture. Flat styles keep cues in the picture plane |
| Visual load | Cumulative demand on attention and space of everything drawn (information, imagery, supporters, containers and edges, treatments, surface, color intensity), judged for the whole against density caps |
| Governing axis / axis ledger / headroom | Dimension groups stack or run along / the one-pass total of each group's extent (load row at the region's width, wraps recorded, spacing steps included), separations, margins, and placed quiet against the axis, as shares of the format / ≥ one separation step unassigned; a ledger that closes only by cutting a spacing step or the quiet does not close |
| Reading sequence | Route over placed regions following the reading path; agrees with attention hierarchy and placement |
| Treatment vocabulary | The few rendering treatments (outline, shadow, gradient, texture, highlight, dimension) the one visual language admits, each with job and roles; none outside it |

## Fit ladder (full)
When content or load exceeds the plan, give way in this order, each rung exhausted first; least meaning first, quiet last:
1. least-purpose items: rendering effects, surface detail, ornament, supporters on or crowding information, supporters beyond essential deficits (atmosphere on ground information does not need yields only after the needed quiet is secured);
2. weight of enclosure: lighter container rung, merge, drop (spacing before rules before boxes);
3. arrangement: grouping, columns, grids, wrapping, a more compact form, and if none fits, the next strategy;
4. treatment: designed text to set text, lowest rank first;
5. sizes above floors, lowest rank first, then hero group and identity unit toward what their role needs (never below the message's need); the hero group reaches its minimum share before any core content is demoted at rung 6; rank steps stay decisive on contrast, isolation, position; no other tier inflated into freed area;
6. stage: demote **reference** content to its later stage; core content only with the hero group at minimum, never below comfortable readability at its nearest stage;
7. optional content, lowest value first;
8. only then the quiet share, to the least that isolates glance content, with a volume risk recorded.

**Applying it:** measure the shortfall once (ledger); compute each rung's **yield** by arithmetic on its rows, never by trial layouts: merging n containers into one = (n − 1) × (2M + L − S); dropping a container = 2M + its adjoining gap; n columns (only where width floors pass) = (rows − ⌈rows ÷ n⌉) × pitch; a tier to its floor = lines × pitch × (1 − floor ÷ size); stage demotion = its rows at the later floor; a spacing step is never a yield (S, M, L: `8_frame_and_space.md` F). Add yields in rung order until the shortfall closes; apply together; re-total once. Trimming one element or drawing another layout is not applying the ladder. If rung 7 leaves a shortfall, take rung 8, record the volume risk, stop.
**Never, at any rung:** shrink a role below its floor; take a spacing step below its floor; compress, stretch, or slant letterforms; delete mandatory content; give core content the deliberate-reading floor; resize one member of an equivalent group; take separation from a neighbor. If mandatory content cannot fit after rung 8, conflict priority decides and the volume risk is reported.
