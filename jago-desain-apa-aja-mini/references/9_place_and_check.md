# Phase 5b: hierarchy, placement, load, balance, capacity, backgrounds, review

**Needs:** everything from `8_frame_and_space.md`; Phase 4 system with purpose lines and weight ceilings; Phase 3 mark role, layering, tone, density, point of tension; the counterweight. **Produces:** the Execution Specification (per element and text item: region, rank, size as share of the format, treatment by Phase 4 name, relationships), background and transition behavior, reproduction adjustments, obligations realized or accepted, flags. The review result goes to the Record.
**Verify once:** one pass over the specified composition: hierarchy → load → balance → capacity (then freeze sizes) → proportion/fill/zone → review. Fit edits and verification do not interleave: close the ledger first, verify after the last fit edit; a later edit that changes no region order, rank, or side does not reopen it. A failure returns to the earliest step that owns it (cap two cycles).

## A. Hierarchy and planes
The sketch already states rank order, route, and balance; this section sets each element's levers to deliver them and verifies once. Number of levels follows the piece.
- **Explicit rank:** record the attention hierarchy per stage as numbered ranks with the levers each holds. Rank follows function (purpose lines). **Exactly one element is first at glance** (a hero group or equal image set counts as one); every element holds a rank or is removed. Same-rank elements share weight levers so they read as a set; same-rank items of different roles differ in form, not weight.
- **Importance vs prominence:** mandatory status and volume are not prominence. A zone's area, a text mass's weight, a container's mass, a utility item's emphasis never exceed what its rank earns. Secondary and utility info is found by position, grouping, a legible tier, not size or weight; contacts and fine print take the lowest prominence that stays findable; essential info (prices, hours, action) is clear by position and legibility without competing with the hero. Large secondary text is contained by compact arrangement and by weight and contrast kept to what legibility needs.
- **Rank separation:** each rank leads the next on ≥ 2 levers, one primary (size, value contrast, isolation, position); a slight step is a hedge: merge or separate. Take the cheapest primary lever first (contrast, isolation, position cost no area; scale costs area). Levers are scarce: strongest contrast, heaviest weight, largest scale, and accent color belong to the first rank only. First-rank pairing is not allowed. Distance within a group is clearly less than between groups.
- **Verification (the one hierarchy check):** predict the attention order from the placed composition, not intent. (1) At each stage count the levers each element holds (area or scale step, value contrast, chroma, weight, isolation, position on the path, container or treatment carried), primary levers first. (2) Order by that count. (3) Predicted order must equal recorded order. Remedy an inversion on the lightest lever; lower an element pulling above its rank on its strongest lever; handle one whose effect does not register by Fulfillment. A secondary element whose compound (text plus container) lands in a higher band is lowered on its container first. A remedy never moves a region: that returns to the sketch, naming the failed check (counts toward the cap).
- **Hierarchy, sequence, placement agree:** the first-rank element is noticed first wherever it sits; the sequence runs from it as a route through adjacent placed regions along the reading path, without doubling back; an item early in attention but late on the path (or reverse) is repositioned or re-ranked. Phase 6 repeats the route so no ordering word contradicts a position word.
- **Fulfillment:** per element, name what limits its stated effect at its stage (a neighbor taking its levers, placement off the path, too little contrast/isolation/scale, a hiding relationship) and remove it by the lightest means: reposition, isolate, separate levers, link, resize within rank. An element that cannot reach its effect within rank and ceiling is re-ranked, re-treated (back to Phase 4 for it), or removed.
- **Who leads:** hero and identity unit (per mark role) lead; evidence imagery and hooks follow; guides, structure, ground stay below the information they serve. The unit's distinction comes from Phase 4 mechanisms, not size; its scale follows its mark role.
- **Connection:** every major element links to ≥ 1 other by a stated link; links never outrank what they connect.
- **Planes** (skip the first two rules for a single plane): order agrees with rank; hero and lead content take the nearest serving plane, supporters on or behind what they serve; an element forward for another reason is held to its ceiling by other means; each plane differs from its neighbor by > 1 cue; only cues surviving the glance reach and the reproduction method carry glance.
- **Overlap** serves depth/rank, integration, connection, or richness, and the covered element keeps its **essential part** (text: every letterform; mark: whole; hero/product: silhouette and proof features; information: what the stage needs); cover only inessential parts. Do not overlap when it hides an essential part, lacks contrast against what it covers, cannot be rendered reliably, is a graze or near-touch that reads as accident, or is only for looks. *Function test:* after the overlap, each element still does its job. **Clearance:** non-overlapping neighbors keep the step their relationship calls for; a gap too small to read as separation or overlap is widened to the next step or overlapped decisively.

## B. Implementing elements
Per element: role, scale, weight, color, treatment, plane, position, the decision it traces to, and **relationships** to nearest neighbors (rail or axis, gap step, relative position, overlap or clearance). No relationship is left to the generator.
- **Equivalent items** share one treatment (size, weight, width, posture, case, color, line spacing) wherever they sit; size encodes role and rank, never length; length is absorbed by width, columns, wrapping, alignment, arrangement; the longest sets the group's fit and the group's width, columns, or container change before any entry's type. Variation only for a real difference in importance, role, emphasis, recorded.
- Type sizes follow stages; prices, calls to action, contact details are functional text, clear at their own stage, not enlarged by default. The unit claims its own region with protected clear space. Specify where every mandatory item sits and its role; integrated items name the part pointed to, side or path, pointer style; attached items name the group. Designed copy keeps its Phase 4 treatment and never reverts to plain text.
- **Supporters** answer their deficit: markers align with the items they label at one size; pointers end on what they name and never cross; a path carries only a given sequence; borders stay inside the safe margin unless bleeding deliberately; a block or band holds one group; a tag attaches to one fact without competing with the hero; a scrim sits behind only its text zone; atmosphere sits where the strategy placed it, below information in contrast and detail. **Placed by host and count** (edge, side, corner), never measured positions; the last thing placed and the first removed if clearance fails.
- A **reserved asset panel** (asset the generator cannot reproduce) gets its own region at needed size (a code sized to scan, with quiet zone), inside the plan, isolated, with a quiet frame in system colors; never a stand-in.
- Figures by Phase 4 role; one design everywhere; a supplied figure never redrawn.

## C. Load, fit ladder, balance
- **Load check (whole composition):** tally by category (text groups, containers and edges, supporters, illustrations, designed and rendering treatments, surface, color intensity) against the Phase 3 density level, with the **density caps** as the pass line for families, container designs, treatments. Fits only with headroom and intact quiet. Count attention claims per stage; beyond what the stage's time can parse, remove or merge the lowest-ranked. Subdivide into no more groups, containers, or repeated forms than the content's structure needs. The axis ledger is the axis check; re-total only if specifying changed an extent. On failure: the ladder, one batch.
- **Renderability:** mandatory text in the at-risk band is first made safer by design (shorter lines, larger size, simpler grouping); the remainder is a rendering risk for the notes.
- **Balance is read off the region table** (mass class and side per region; nothing measured). Sum classes by side, center regions half to each. Symmetric/radial: left and right differ by ≤ 1 point. Asymmetric equilibrium or deliberate tension: the heavier side is the one the sketch names, a named counterweight answers it, difference ≤ 2. Vertically, an upper half > 1 point above the lower is top-heavy and needs the sketch's reason (e.g., hero cropped at the top). Beyond that: unbalanced. **Remedy, least new weight first:** (1) rebalance within ranks (lighten heavy / strengthen light by intensity or area, resize the hero within rank); (2) deploy the Phase 4 counterweight (weight from area and distance, linked, below every rank, never carrying required info); (3) swap sides or shift an axis only where the route survives, else return to the sketch; (4) no counterweight in the system → Phase 4. No remedy takes area from quiet or separation; re-check additions against the shares.

## D. Capacity check (before sizes freeze), proportion, fill, zone
- **Capacity (one pass, after balance):** only strings in doubt (near a container's width, any at-risk string), longest of each group first: width = characters × cap × width factor (≈ 0.75 to 0.9 text; up to ≈ 1.0 heavy or wide display) against the inner width after padding, with headroom (a string that only just fits does not fit: a render short of room compresses or skews letters). The ledger's wrap column already counted lines. On failure apply the ladder as a batch (arrangement rungs first) or take the next surviving strategy; remedies act on the group or role, never one member, and letterform proportions are never a remedy. **Then** record final sizes and planned wraps of every multi-line string and each group's extent as a share of the governing dimension; the prompt uses these.
- **Proportion:** reduce any role or group far above its minimum that is not the hero or glance message (as a whole); zone area ratios agree with rank; sizes in a zone stand in clear steps, never near-equal.
- **Fill:** every quiet area has a job or is reassigned in the plan; it is never filled with an item or taken later by decoration (which lives in atmosphere fields and edges). Uniform, tonal, or fine-grain ground runs through it; visible pattern does not.
- **Zone:** internal spacing is the step its text calls for; content sits inside its region with resting room; a zone needing more room finds it within its own content, never by taking separation; open ground elsewhere does not compensate.

## E. Backgrounds, reproduction, the set
- **Backgrounds and transitions:** continuity and contrast across zones, quiet where information lives; realize the Phase 4 ground, scrim, edge behavior, transitions, surface only where decided, subordinate to hierarchy; denser content, calmer ground; no ground effect softens text or hero edges.
- **Reproduction:** apply what the medium reading imposes, carrying forward only what changes the look or generation. Where the medium can lose or cover the edge, keep mandatory content, the unit, and fine borders inside the safe margin (a deliberate bleed meets none). Bleed is visual direction only, never guides or marks in the prompt. Physical pieces add viewing distance per stage, ambient light, material and finish, handling, format behavior (folds, panels, curvature), trim tolerance.
- **Across the set:** shared identity once, with the identity form each deliverable uses and one figure design; a distinct purpose per deliverable; consistent treatment; per-deliverable stage assignment, space plan, strategy, prompt.
