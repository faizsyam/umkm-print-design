# Standards, decision protocol, and the Design Log (all phases)

**Read:** after the summary is confirmed; §1 on any collision, §3 for Direction decisions, §4 for the log.

## 1. Conflict priority and non-negotiables
**Conflict priority** (a lower item yields only to a higher one and keeps its closest surviving expression): (1) factual integrity, mandatory content, exclusions, fixed assets; (2) perception and comprehension under real conditions for the full audience (low vision, color-vision differences), by restructuring, rescaling, or regrouping, never by deleting mandatory content; (3) user's stated requirements and qualities; (4) the communication objective and strategy (differentiation, departure), only among options keeping 3 intact; (5) established brand identity not supplied as an asset; (6) creative ambition. Within 2, space yields last (fit ladder). A named choice that weakens the objective stays: mitigate inside it and offer the concern once as a labeled suggestion.
**Non-negotiables:** no invented facts, and no implied claim, endorsement, or authority the subject lacks · user text verbatim, typos flagged not fixed · supplied logos/characters never redesigned, restyled, simplified, recolored; no unasked figure; never a real person's likeness · no copying competitors or existing designs · no identifier that is only a styled name (unless supplied or requested as plain text) · no placeholders or blank zones for content the skill should create (sole exception: a reserved asset panel for a supplied asset the generator cannot reproduce: a scannable code, a photo or logo file that cannot be attached; designed into the composition, flagged, never imitated) · no stated quality contradicted by a decision or left without a visible carrier · no suggestion enters the brief or design without the user's yes · no unfamiliar jargon or walls of text for the user · no workflow output beyond the defined messages.

## 2. Principles
1. **Whole-system thinking.** Judge every consequential decision against the whole: role, relationships, effect on hierarchy, space, communication, what it constrains downstream. A decision optimized alone or taken from a template is unfinished.
2. **Structured exploration.** Consequential decisions go through the protocol (§3); trivial ones directly. A familiar solution is admissible only after beating alternatives derived from this problem.
3. **Evidence-informed.** Keep given, researched, inferred, creative-choice apart with tags; an inference carries its reasoning.
4. **Contextual specificity.** Justify every direction by this subject and deliverable; one that works unchanged for a peer is unfinished. A look-shaping attribute no decision names is filled by the model's apparent value, so specificity must reach every one.
5. **Functional creativity and restraint.** Novelty names its purpose. Every element carries a purpose line and must be worth its marginal cost against the next-lighter alternative (nothing, spacing, a smaller form). Neither minimalism nor maximalism is a default; using a resource and leaving it out are both reasoned decisions. Restraint removes what has no purpose; it does not excuse omitting what the piece lacks (interest, character, evidence of the offer, mood, scanning aids, material, the theme's world). Reduction never costs character: the fewer the elements, the more each carries (depth, an ownable trait, a link). Decoration never outranks the information it serves, and richness is bounded by the density caps (`5_plan.md`): in a dense plan, character comes from depth in the hero and lettering, not more families.
6. **Explicit rank, planned space, cumulative load.** Every element holds a stated rank, separated by contrast the viewer cannot miss; importance is not prominence (prominence = area, scale, contrast, weight, isolation, position, container, as drawn, and follows rank). Space is planned from content needs then spent top-down (format → groups → elements → text tiers); a group's share is never below its need or above its rank's ceiling. Quiet is genuinely open, local (judged at each zone's perimeter), protected, and never spent on enlarging elements. Crowding is a fit failure resolved by the ladder, never by shrinking below floors or distorting letters. Load is judged cumulatively.
7. **Internal consistency.** Brand, theme, style, color, type, mark, imagery, composition reinforce each other; one primary visual language leads and secondary treatments are few, named, applied by role. Group by job and referent, not only by reading time; text that labels, prices, explains, or substantiates a depicted thing is composed with it (unless the hero carries the message alone). Form shows relation (sequence, comparison, hierarchy) read from content. Hierarchy, sequence, and placement agree through the final prompt; relationships are specified, not implied. Equivalent items share one treatment.
8. **Fidelity to the brief.** Mandatory content, user text, exclusions, fixed assets, qualities, priorities are never silently overridden, even by the skill's strategy. Seek distinction inside constraints by committing fully, never by diluting or evading.
9. **Effort follows impact.** Deliberate longest on mark, core idea, hero, space plan (measured once, in shares of the format, not found by trial layouts; a fit failure is repaired by the ladder as one batch, never by redrawing); brisk on routine decisions. A decision is closed when its decisive criterion is stated and no recorded check fails it; it reopens only by naming a failed check. What an earlier decision fixes is applied, not decided again.
10. **Context before convention.** Universal principles hold everywhere; what a medium imposes, its audience relies on, and it affords are derived in Phase 2. Keep a convention the audience relies on wherever departing costs effort or credibility; spend distinction on expression and identity.

## 3. Decision classes and the protocol
| Class | Decisions | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Direction | core idea, style, hero (P3); new mark/lockup, created lead figure (P4, extended development); spatial strategy (P5) | §3 protocol, sized by openness |
| System | P4: palette and value key, type roles, treatment grade and device per content role, shape and container language, supporter policy, ground, derived from Direction as one decision | one compact comparison |
| Execution | details of a copy treatment (breaks, emphasis, small devices), non-mandatory wording, small art, icons, edge details, decorative placement by host and count | specified directly in the Phase 6 prompt: no log item, comparison, or purpose line |
| Escalation | any System/Execution choice with a named risk: conflicts with a quality or fixed asset, exact text at renderability risk, contradicts an earlier decision, user doubted it | compact comparison for that choice only |
Phase 2's differentiation, weakest link, departure take a compact comparison when open, else are decided directly. Phases 1 and 6 hold no exploration.
**Openness.** Fixed: record as fixed, no cards. Steered: run on the open remainder only, ≥ 2 candidates. Open: ≥ 3. Content volume, crowding, several deliverables never raise openness; they raise load and fit work. Never reduced: apparent option, runner-up, decisive criterion, quality checks, load budget test, capacity check, verbatim content, review, audits. Re-read openness if a phase shows the brief leaves more open than assumed.

| Scope by openness | Open | Steered |
|---|---|---|
| Research | each question that can change the decision | the one that can |
| Territory survey | 4 territories, 3 source families | 2 (named lineage as the apparent option + one beyond), open dimensions only |
| Cards | every field | fields that tell candidates apart + quality compatibility |
| Principles (P3) | compared: space intent with density/rhythm, imagery/decoration, integration, mark role, layering, tone/positioning | compared: first four; rest direct |
| New mark/figure | ≥ 6 seeds, all checks recorded | ≥ 4 seeds, pass/fail lines; if the hero/core idea fixes the form class: 2-3 seeds, 2 candidates on device and lockup only |

**Inheritance.** What an earlier decision fixes (corner geometry, type role, palette role, treatment vocabulary, the hero's footprint and form, a mark's form class) is applied, not compared. Decisions reading each other on one group share one criterion.

**Direction protocol:**
1. **Name the apparent option** as benchmark and state the pressure the decision resolves (a requirement, tension, the weakest link). Derive candidates from the pressure, not the category.
2. **Write candidates** (count by openness): at most one apparent, at least one from beyond it; they differ on a structural axis and have different sources (product truth, audience moment, environment, differentiator, name/origin, material, inventory, survey). Fill all card fields in specialist terms before judging. Drop a card after one line if its depiction mode is ineligible or it fails a hard constraint (footprint over budget, fit screen).
3. **Judge** last-written first. Eliminate any card contradicting a quality or mandatory content (unless §1 forces the trade). Rate survivors only where they differ, on lenses that discriminate: qualities; environment (neighbors, light, distance, departure/fit); capacity and production (load fit, material, renderability); coherence with decisions made; brand, audience, culture. One lens is not an evaluation.
4. **Challenge once:** name the runner-up, the strongest case against the leader, and the leader's margin as one decisive criterion naming an observable property. If none can be stated after step 5's rebuild, the candidates tie: take the lowest space and rendering cost, record the tie. An apparent option wins only by naming the criterion the others failed.
5. **Test the leader.** *Predictable?* A stranger could guess it from category and place → rebuild once, retest; a second failure takes the best resolution, recorded (a convention the audience relies on stays). *Specific?* Nameable by lineage and traits from this brief, fails unchanged for a competitor. *Ambitious?* A merely competent card beats an ambitious one only on a stated criterion.
6. **Engineer:** each material weakness gets a named move or accepted cost; each key strength a place it is amplified. Select, transplant a strength, repair a weakness, or combine as a hybrid.
7. **Record** in the log: Specification, Source, Basis, Rejected (apparent, runner-up, decisive criterion, what was kept); mitigations, accepted costs, amplify-here moves as carry-forward obligations. A missing field means the decision is not made.

**Compact comparison:** name the apparent option; compare ≥ 2 alternatives (3 where real) differing in logic, one line each on quality compatibility, environment, coherence; choose, state the decisive criterion and the weakness handled. Where evidence leaves one option, say so; manufacture none. The System decision compares two whole-system variants only where the direction leaves a real fork; otherwise derive it, one line per part.

**Specialist terms** (use a term only if definable; an undefinable prestigious word is vagueness): style = lineage and defining traits; imagery = depiction mode, lineage, proportions, linework, technique, texture, detail, color treatment, stylization, light, finish; color = hue family, value structure, chroma, temperature, harmony, area proportion; type = voice, classification, stroke contrast, proportion, x-height, width, spacing, alignment, orientation; composition = axis, alignment, grid logic, figure-ground, negative-space shape, layering, focal hierarchy, weight, tension; decoration = motif family, repeat logic, scale, density, contrast, placement; reproduction = ink, substrate, finish, display.

## 4. Workflow rules and the Design Log
**Order.** Phase N+1 starts only when N's decisions are made and its hand-off check passed; no phase skipped, merged, or deferred; a rich brief shortens exploration, never a phase. A conclusion held early is a hypothesis for its own phase.
**Hand-offs stay narrow.** A phase reads S and the decisions it names, not candidates, rejected options, or rationale; a reason a later phase must honor travels as a carry-forward obligation. Later phases apply decisions, not re-weigh them; refining decisions (budget, provisional ranks, groups) travel provisional and the refining phase re-derives them.
**Traceability.** Every decision names its source; one with none was made from habit. Every quality traces translation → carrier → prompt clause. Every obligation is realized or recorded as accepted cost.
**Gate integrity.** State no number or decision to the user before the checks that could change it have run. A passed check re-runs only when a decision it reads changed.
**Return, then re-run forward.** A failed check goes to the earliest responsible phase; redo in order only the decisions that read the changed one (dependents whose inputs did not materially change are re-checked, not redecided). Cap each issue and every loop at two cycles, then take the best resolution and record it. Never patch the prompt for an upstream cause.
**Notes.** Record decisions and reasons, not deliberation; a figure once, where first derived. State a measurement only if a decision depends on it. A passing check is one line. **Units:** after Phase 2's floors every size and region is a share of the format. **Log economy:** D holds Specification and Source per decision; Basis and Rejected only for Direction and Escalation decisions.

**Ownership (who decides what, reading what).**
| Phase | Decides | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ledger, deliverable set, translations, openness | user input |
| 2 | profile with floors, audience encounter, differentiation, inventory; problem: weakest link, tensions, message, groups, load table | S, research |
| 3 | plan criteria (budget); core idea → style → hero (each reads the one before); principles, carrier map | S, problem, load table, profile, inventory, survey |
| 4 | system decision; new mark/lockup, created figure | style dims 1, 4-7, fixed assets, profile, stages, carrier map, hero, principles, groups |
| 5 | per deliverable: stages → groups/needs → composition (region table) → space plan → ranks, placement, balance, capacity freeze → backgrounds | S format, message, groups, load table, system, hero hosting and footprint, integration stance, balance type, space intent |
| 6 | prompt set, each prompt | S, D, Execution Specifications |

**The Design Log** (`/home/claude/design_log.md`, compact English; without file tools, keep in notes) is the only state between phases and turns. Create it when the user confirms the summary; append per phase at hand-off; read S and D when a turn resumes. It is never presented except under the trace option in `SKILL.md`. If lost, rebuild from the summary, entries, prompts, user messages, marked provisional.
- **S** (standing block): see `1_intake_and_brief.md` §6; Phase 3 adds the quality carrier map; otherwise S changes only by user corrections.
- **D**: one line per decision a later phase reads, with fields: **Decision** (name) · **Specification** (prompt-ready wording: traits as seen, no rationale, dimensions as shares of the format) · **Source** (provenance and origin) · **Basis** (principle number or evidence; Direction/Escalation only) · **Rejected** (Direction only: runner-up, decisive criterion, alternatives) · **Status** (current, stale, re-derived). Plus carry-forward obligations and open flags (kept apart so nothing unresolved reads as settled). A decision fixed by the user, an asset, or evidence logs Source fixed, no Rejected. Basis and Rejected are read only by checks, reviews, audits, revisions.
- **Stale flag:** when a decision changes, set dependents stale transitively and re-derive in phase order; one whose inputs did not materially change is re-checked and set current.
