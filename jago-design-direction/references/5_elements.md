# Phase 5 (continued): elements, brand presence, and checks

**Needs:** the parts in `5_system.md`, the theme record, the brand record, S, the interpretation. **Produces:** the rest of the **direction record**. Section numbers continue from `5_system.md`.

## 3. Elements and brand presence
- **Information presentation (every group and element is derived, not styled by habit).** For each group and each visual element, decide in this order and record one line:
  1. **Job:** its communication purpose: what the viewer understands or does because of it (from the groups and tiers).
  2. **Rank:** its importance to the objective from the content map (primary, supporting, reference), kept distinct from its tier; what it leads, what it follows, what it must stay beside (items that label, price, or explain a depicted thing belong with it).
  3. **Form:** how it is represented: words, a pictogram, a depiction, or a picture paired with words. The user's lines fix what is communicated, never how it looks. Choose the form that delivers the unit to this viewer with least effort, and **name the reason**: words alone for a unit whose kind is picturable need one as much as a picture does.
     - *A picture earns its place* where the unit's kind (an item, channel, place, function) is recognized faster seen than read, where it helps the eye find the unit among others, or where it adds appeal or understanding words cannot.
     - *Words keep* the content itself (names, claims, conditions, prices, specifics, identifiers) and emphasis. Where a picture can carry the kind and words the specifics, pair them: the picture leads, the words follow, and a label naming only the kind of data gives way to its pictogram while its value and clarifier stay words.
     - *A picture is dropped* where redundant (the content already identifies itself and nothing competes to be found), ambiguous (it could mean several things), or beyond what the maker draws dependably.
     - *Depictions and pictograms:* each item a list or offer names is a candidate for its own depiction, which shows the kind of thing and never asserts quantity or specification; a pictogram is a recognizable symbol of its kind, carries no lettering, and follows the brand's shape logic.
     - *Binding:* a visual stays bound to the unit it represents; a marker repeated unchanged on every row decorates instead of informing, so it carries information or is dropped.
  4. **Weight:** its visual weight, set from its rank through the means available: the strength of its ground against the field, scale, richness of treatment, contrast of its text, and the space around it. Primary groups take the strongest means; supporting groups recede through a quieter ground, lower contrast, smaller scale, and more air; reference groups sit quiet but findable, in a stable place with safe legibility, never hidden. Equal ranks share a weight; a lower rank never carries a ground or richness equal to a higher one because one theme device is applied to all.
  5. **Theme expression:** how the concept shows in it (which carrier), so it belongs to the theme's world instead of sitting on it.
  6. **Place:** its zone, depth, position on the route, and interlock.
  7. **Treatment:** the artistic and structural form its lettering, grouping device, and rendering take, drawn from the lettering voice, color roles, shape logic, and imagery set above, so no element needs a new invention.
  Treatments differ by role inside one grammar: equal items share one design; items of different roles differ in weight and form, not size alone; no element takes a treatment because its neighbor has one. **Grouping devices:** the lightest first (spacing and alignment, then a rule or tonal step, then a container); a container needs a reason the lighter ones fail. Any device that exists is built from the brand's shape logic and the theme's structure, never a default rectangle or pill, and a device is shared only by units of one role and rank; containers holding different roles differ at a glance.
- **Brand presence:**
  - *The mark:* a supplied logo is exact; a new mark follows the brand record's narration, construction, and lockup, its letters complete and in order. It stands as a distinct object with its own clear field, never overlapped by the hero, lettering, or supporters, never fused with them or restyled. Its tier follows its job in the interpretation: glance where recognition drives the action, otherwise a close-tier signature.
  - *The language:* visible with the mark covered: constants honored, the flex used as the theme directs, the signatures deployed within their scopes.
- **Ownable traits (one to three):** chosen, not invented: from the brand's signatures and the theme's devices, the ones that recur here and make the piece recognizable, never the category's stock devices. Each is drawable and dependable for the maker (no exact paths or counts) and states its scope: where it appears and where it never does (never across mandatory text or the first read unless that is the trait). A trait applied everywhere is a filter. More than three dilute identity.
- **Guards:** failures the user would also call failures, taken from the prior read's Fix list, each stated as its positive opposite. A guard that names an unwanted object invites it: name an object only when the maker commits it unprompted (extra text, watermarks, invented marks). At most three guards beyond the user's exclusions and the text rule. Never a guard that removes what the user's own words welcome, or that blocks richness the quality requires.
- **Tensions:** confirm each lead side against the assembled direction and record what the other side keeps through structure.

## 4. Direction check (once)
Run the picture review (`4_theme.md` §5) on the assembled direction. Then:
- **Ancestry:** does every part name a parent in the theme, the brand, or a mandate, and does the chain predict itself?
- **Cover test:** cover the mark: is it still this brand? Cover the text: does it still do this design's job?
- **Family test:** pick any two elements: do they read as one hand, treated by role and not alike?
- **Weight test:** order the groups by rank, then by visual weight (ground, contrast, scale, richness, space): the orders match, equal only where rank is equal, and every receding group is still findable and readable.
- Does the picture beat the prior read on a named visible dimension, and does every part serve a named item of keep, raise, or fix? Does every user quality have two carriers on the dominant masses at its intensity, and does any decision signal its opposite? Does anything read as a literal material or object where a graphic form was meant? Are the ownable traits present in the hero, the lettering, and the supporting system, within their scopes? Does anything fixed here belong to the maker (§5)?
A conflict returns to the responsible part, or to the layer that caused it; two cycles at most.

## 5. What the direction leaves to the maker
Exact placement, sizes, spacing, type pairing within the voice, the exact position of lights, the position and count of small decorative elements, finish detail below the ownable traits. The direction fixes intent, relations, and defining traits, never a specification.

## Gate (fails if any is false)
The chain holds and every part names its parent and reason · the art direction passes its eight tests · the composition architecture covers focal structure, scale and weight, interlock, depth, zones, balance, and route · the hero has a situation and depicts only what is true · every group and element has job, rank, form (with its reason), weight, theme expression, place, and treatment; visual weights follow the rank order; no group shares a zone or device with another role only for being low in rank · the mark stands apart and whole · every picture mandate is answered by id, every quality and tension is carried at its intensity · the picture review and the direction check passed · nothing is fixed that belongs to the maker. Then append the record.
