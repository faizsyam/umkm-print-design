# Phase 3a: create and choose the direction

**Needs:** S; the Phase 2 record (design truth, code map and baseline, opportunity pool, problem, tensions). **Produces:** the chosen idea, its runner-up and decisive criterion, and the obligations it carries. Phase 3b (`4_direction_system.md`) develops it into the full direction. **Guided:** show the cards (§5) and stop for the choice; Phase 3b runs after it.

## 1. Frame
State the design problem in one sentence: "make [business] unmistakably itself to [audience], in [context], so that they [response]", with the lead side of each tension. Everything below answers that sentence.

## 2. Openness
Fixed: record, no cards. Steered: explore the open remainder, at least 2 cards. Open: at least 3. Volume and crowding never raise openness; they raise feasibility work. A look the user fixed is realized with specificity: distinctiveness then lives in the idea, the signature moves, the hero's defining traits, and the structure of the content. Never reduced: naming the baseline, the runner-up, the decisive criterion, and the tests in §4.

## 3. Build the cards
1. **Select 3 or 4 seeds** from the pool (fewer if Steered) that differ on a **structural axis**: what carries identity, what the viewer does first, how information is structured, register, image-making method. Color or ornament alone do not make cards distinct. At most one card stays close to the baseline; at least one comes from a lens beyond it.
2. **Translate each seed into visual structure.** A truth becomes a form logic, a behavior (how elements relate or are arranged), a material, a viewpoint or scale, or a use of the content's own shape: never a picture of the thing itself, which is the baseline. Ask of each seed: what would this truth look like as a structure, if no object from the trade appeared?
3. **Develop each into a card**, every field in specialist terms (a label is not a card):
   - **Idea:** one sentence joining a true thing about the business to something the audience recognizes. A mood word or a category is not an idea.
   - **The true thing** and **the recognition** it relies on.
   - **Signature moves:** one to three recurring, ownable devices, each something a maker could draw (a form logic, a color behavior, a lettering treatment, a structural gesture, a way of using the content's own shape). Without them the card is a mood.
   - **Look:** four to six defining traits: lineage, how forms and lines are built, surface and finish, light, color logic, lettering voice.
   - **Hero** and **how it carries the hierarchy:** what leads, how content groups, where space stays open.
   - **Breaks and keeps:** which codes from the map it breaks, bends, keeps.
   - **Risk and move**, **fit with each quality**.
4. **Craft safety:** an idea whose success depends on something that cannot be depicted accurately (exact data, many counted forms, fine detail, a likeness) is rebuilt around what can be shown.

## 4. Judge and choose
Eliminate any card that contradicts a quality, a mandatory item, or the conflict priority. Judge the survivors on the seven tests, only where they differ:
| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| **Ownable** | With the name removed, does it still point to this business? Does it fail the swap test for a named peer? |
| **Relevant** | Does it express the business's real character, positioning, price tier, and audience, and every stated quality? |
| **Memorable** | Can it be recalled in one sentence, and does one signature survive without the piece in front of you? |
| **Coherent** | Does every part of the system follow from the idea (the chain in `4_direction_system.md`), with nothing contradicting it? |
| **Effective** | Does the message register at glance, is there exactly one first read with a decisive contrast (scale, value, density, or form) that gives it presence at the farthest distance, and can the content hierarchy be carried at the stated distance and format? |
| **Fresh within fit** | Does it break the codes it can afford to break and keep those the audience relies on? Is there at most one deliberate risk, with a mitigation? |
| **Truthful** | Is every claim, symbol, and depiction true and respectful for this audience? |
Then:
1. **Compare with the baseline:** the leader must differ from it on at least three named dimensions (form logic, color behavior, composition logic, imagery mode, lettering voice, structure of information). If it differs only in adjectives, or a stranger could guess the idea from the category and place, it is the baseline; rebuild once from another seed.
2. **Challenge once:** name the runner-up and the strongest case against the leader. The leader's margin is one decisive criterion naming an observable property. If none can be stated, the cards tie: take the simpler, record the tie.
3. **Engineer:** each material weakness gets a named move or an accepted cost; each key strength a place where it is amplified. Select, transplant a strength, repair, or combine as a **hybrid** (candidates under one logic, statable as one idea); a compromise needing "and also" is rejected.
4. **Record:** the idea, signature moves, runner-up, decisive criterion, what was kept from the others, obligations.

## 5. Guided entry (cards)
Show only the choice, its reason, and the alternatives, in the user's language, skimmable in seconds, with no internal terms for a new user.
```
**Step 3 of 4: creative direction**
What I learned: [one or two lines: the business's truth, the surroundings, how it can stand out]
| Direction | What it looks like | Why / trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **[name]**: [idea, ≤ 12 words] | [2 or 3 traits, incl. the signature] | [reason tied to this business] · [weakness] |
| [name] | ... | ... |
You said "…" → carried as: …
OK, or tell me what to change?
```
Two or three rows, the chosen one first; matters already fixed by the user go in one line "Already settled: …". Feedback: acknowledge in one line, rerun what it touches, show only changed rows as Before | After, ask once; after two rounds without agreement choose the best resolution, say so, continue. A question is answered in one or two plain sentences, then the confirmation line repeats. Feedback contradicting a stated quality or fact: say so kindly and offer the closest option keeping both.

## Gate (fails if any is false)
Every open decision shows its baseline, runner-up, and decisive criterion · the cards differ on a structural axis and at least one comes from beyond the baseline · no card derives from a competitor · the leader passed the seven tests and differs from the baseline on three named dimensions · every weakness has a move or an accepted cost · the idea fits one sentence and carries one to three signature moves. Then proceed to `4_direction_system.md`.
