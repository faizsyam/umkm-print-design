# Phase 3b: the picture as a system

**Needs:** the chosen picture and its record; S; the Phase 2 record. **Produces:** the **direction record**: the picture as narrated, art direction at ceiling, composition architecture, hero, ownable traits, color logic, lettering, supporting richness, identity decisions, guards, and how each tension is carried.

## 1. The chain
A distinctive direction is one logic seen everywhere. The chosen picture leads; the parts are developed in this order, each stating in one clause **because of what** it is so and each reading the ones before it. A part that cannot name its reason is a default and is replaced.
**picture → art direction → composition architecture → hero → ownable traits → color logic → lettering → supporting richness → identity → guards**
The forces in §2 set the language; the picture is realized through the language, never the reverse. Where they conflict, re-express the picture before bending the language. The art direction governs the rest: color follows the rendering mode, lettering shares the carrier's construction logic, composition follows how the lineage treats space and depth. Test the chain once at the end: pick any part and predict the next from the picture and the parts before it; if the prediction fails, the part does not belong.

## 2. The parts
- **Art direction** (the most consequential decision for any image-led piece; derived, never merely named; start from the chosen candidate's Look, then test and complete it):
  1. *Carrier:* what the eye reads first (a character, a photograph, a pattern, the lettering). Its language is built first; everything else conforms to it.
  2. *Forces:* the audience's visual world and the appeal bar; the business's tier and promise; each mood quality turned into form consequences at the user's intensity (shape, proportion, rhythm, contrast, saturation, line behavior); the carrier's job; the reproduction and what the maker renders dependably.
  3. *Lineage:* the specific tradition, discipline, or school, as narrow as era, region, and technique allow, whose internal logic answers those forces. State what is adopted from its logic (how it builds form, models light, handles edges, uses color and space, ranks detail) and what is left behind. Adopt its **richness logic** together with its simplifications: a lineage is chosen for the appeal of the finished work in it, never for ease of rendering, and "simple" is an outcome of that logic, never a goal. One lineage leads; a second may govern one axis under a stated rule; a pile of unrelated looks fails. A medium or format word (vector, 3D, photographic, flat) is a production class, not a lineage. Never a living artist, studio, or protected character.
  4. *Ceiling axes:* form construction (geometry, proportion, silhouette); line (presence, weight behavior, terminals, color); **color and rendering** (how deeply forms are modelled: flat, tonal, or fully modelled; how light falls and how soft its shading is; highlight and shadow logic; edge treatment; saturation and temperature relations; the **value key**); surface (graphic marks only; a finish is named only where it is how a depicted thing looks, never as a metaphor for the concept); light; detail by rank (the carrier fullest, the rest stepping down). State every axis at the finish level of the appeal bar, in the richest register the audience's world and the maker's strength allow. Choose the plain option on an axis only for a named failure it avoids, or because the lineage's own logic is plain there.
  5. *Tests:* **Strike** (remove medium and format words and bare mood adjectives; a drawable look remains) · **Kinship** (two skilled makers given the paragraph produce recognizably related work) · **Coherence** (every axis follows from the lineage; none cancels another) · **Home turf** (it sits in the visual world the audience already finds appealing) · **Mood** (each quality has form-level carriers on the dominant masses) · **Tool** (the maker renders it dependably; a concretely described rich look usually renders well, so complexity alone never justifies the plainer choice) · **Ceiling** (every axis is at or above the bar; a viewer would rank the result with the best work in this world, not the average).
- **Composition architecture** (the decision that most separates professional from amateur work; stated as zones and relations in plain words, never as numbers):
  - *Focal structure:* the first read, the contrast that makes it decisive, and the second read.
  - *Scale ladder:* how group sizes step by tier.
  - *Interlock:* how the hero and the headline lockup belong to one composition, and how the hero leads the eye into the offer (its gesture, gaze, or direction; overlap, tucking behind, or a shared contour, whichever the lineage suits). Stacked blocks are the failure.
  - *Depth:* what sits in front and behind, and why.
  - *Zones:* where each group lives relative to the hero and to the others, and what stays clear.
  - *Balance:* the stance and its counterweight; where calm sits so that richness reads as richness.
  - *Route:* the eye's path from first read to action, led by natural cues, not drawn lines.
  - *Edges:* nothing the viewer needs near the trim.
  Where format and load allow one sensible arrangement, state it; where several work equally, state the logic and leave the rest to the maker.
- **Hero and imagery:** what the hero truly is, down to its specific features; the depiction mode follows what must be true: where the viewer must see exactly what is offered, a mode that stylizes it away is ineligible. A hero is chosen only when one subject deserves primary attention and can be depicted truthfully; otherwise there is none, or a small equal set. Give the hero a **situation**: a pose and gesture that express the user's qualities and do a job in the composition (what it points at, looks at, holds, leans toward). A figure (mascot, character) is decided at identity level: kind, character, three to five distinctive features that read at distance, proportions, consistency across images; its simplification and rendering are the art direction's output. Features the maker knows from the subject's kind are left to it. A supplied figure is never redesigned. Depict the specific thing, never a generic glyph interchangeable between subjects.
- **Ownable traits (one to three):** recurring devices that make the piece recognizable and ownable, derived from the picture and the true specifics, never from the category's stock devices: a character trait, a form logic, a color behavior, a lettering construction, a structural gesture. Each is drawable and dependable for the maker (no exact paths or counts) and states its scope: where it appears and where it never does (never across mandatory text or the first read unless that is the trait). A trait applied everywhere is a filter. More than three dilute identity.
- **Color logic:** state what color does here (organize, signal, separate, evoke), then roles and rough proportions (a dominant field, a supporting field, an accent, an ink for text), harmony, temperature, and the **value key**: the light or dark balance by area, ground included, which must support every quality. Depth comes from tints and shade relationships derived from the user's palette, never from leaving it. The strongest chroma goes to the first read; area is not prominence. Colors the user named are fixed; text color is chosen for legibility; hex only for supplied brand colors.
- **Lettering:** the headline, name, and prices are **designed objects**, not type set beside a picture. State their voice, built from the art direction's logic: construction (weight, width, terminals, rhythm), and how each sits in the picture (outline, depth, tilt, color relation to the hero) at the finish level of the bar. Roles that must differ (display, informational, numerals, small print) differ by more than size. Items of one role share one treatment whatever their length. Letters are never compressed, stretched, or slanted to fit. No font names unless a brand requires one.
- **Supporting richness:** every element has a job (function, what is lost without it, effect); two with one function merge. Order of enrichment: deepen the hero and lettering, then interlock the major elements, then atmosphere that belongs to the world, then small supporters. **Organization, not count, limits richness:** every addition shares the system's form and color logic, ranks below the first read, and stays out of the zones of exact text. A stated quality of energy, abundance, richness, or warmth sets a **minimum**: the first read and the ground both carry it at that intensity, and plainness against such a quality fails. An element present only because pieces of this kind have it is replaced by something specific to this business or removed; the audience's own signifiers (appeal bar, *meet*) are not defaults.
- **Containers and grouping devices:** the lightest device first (spacing and alignment, then a rule or tonal step, then a container); a container needs a reason the lighter ones fail. One shape language shared by all containers, rules, and supporters; containers holding different roles differ at a glance; equal items share one design.
- **Identity:** a supplied logo or wordmark is exact. A new one is decided as an **idea**: an ownable, grounded device tying the name to the business's truth (never a styled name alone), weighed against one alternative; its construction is left to the maker, its letters stay complete and in order, and it is distinct from the surrounding lettering by form, color role, scale, or isolation. If the hero or picture already fixes the form, only the device is open.
- **Guards:** failures the user would also call failures, taken from the prior read's Fix list, each stated as its positive opposite. A guard that names an unwanted object invites it: name an object only when the maker commits it unprompted (extra text, watermarks, invented marks). At most three guards beyond the user's exclusions and the text rule. Never a guard that removes what the user's own words welcome, or that blocks richness the quality requires.
- **Tensions:** confirm each lead side against the assembled direction and record what the other side keeps through structure.

## 3. Direction check (once)
Run the picture review (`3_envision.md` §5) on the assembled direction. Then: is the chain unbroken, and does every part serve a named item of the prior read (keep, raise, fix)? Does the picture beat the prior read on a named visible dimension? Does every user quality have two carriers on the dominant masses at its intensity, and does any decision signal its opposite? Does anything read as a literal material or object where a graphic form was meant? Are the ownable traits present in the hero, the lettering, and the supporting system, within their scopes? Does anything fixed here belong to the maker (§4)? A conflict returns to the responsible part; two cycles at most.

## 4. What the direction leaves to the maker
Exact placement, sizes, spacing, type pairing within the voice, the exact position of lights, the position and count of small decorative elements, finish detail below the ownable traits. The direction fixes intent, relations, and defining traits, never a specification.

## Gate (fails if any is false)
The chain holds and every part names its reason · the art direction passes its seven tests · the composition architecture covers focal structure, scale, interlock, depth, zones, balance, and route · the hero has a situation and depicts only what is true · every quality and tension is carried at its intensity · the picture review passed · nothing is fixed that belongs to the maker. Then append the record.
