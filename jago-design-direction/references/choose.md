# Choosing among options (shared by Phases 3 and 4)

**Used for:** the brand design (`3_brand.md`) and the theme (`4_theme.md`). Each supplies its frame, structural axes, candidate fields, review, added ranking criterion, and card. This file holds the method, stated once.

## 1. Openness
- **Fixed** (the user or an asset decides): record it, show no cards, still derive what the user left unsaid.
- **Steered:** explore the open remainder: at least 2 candidates, one the **spine candidate** (the user's stated direction at its best craft, nothing added).
- **Open:** at least 3.
Volume and crowding never raise openness; they raise feasibility work. Never reduced: the prior read, the runner-up, the decisive criterion, and the judging in §4.

## 2. What qualifies as an idea
An idea is judged by how the finished work looks, never by how clever its premise is: it is what a viewer would tell a friend, not a rationale. It qualifies only if:
- the viewer need not learn its rationale for it to work;
- the maker renders it dependably (exact paths, exact counts, precise alignment, fine repeated detail, and small lettering inside a mark are rebuilt around what can be shown);
- it deepens the relation between the parts instead of adding a layer on top of them;
- it is a graphic or situational structure, never a material or an object-as-surface.
Under a fixed spine the idea may be modest: the spine at full craft plus one integrated moment. A premise that competes with the first read or the user's look is eliminated.

## 3. Build the candidates
1. **Select 2 to 4 seeds** from the raw material, the mandates, and the layer above. A governing idea grows from the spine, the distinctive truth, or the layer's own job. Look past the category's habitual answer: a seed may join facts that seem unrelated when the join is true, relevant, and visible.
2. Candidates differ on a **structural axis** named by the layer file; color or ornament alone never make candidates distinct. At most one stays close to the prior read.
3. **Develop each in specialist terms** (a label is not a candidate): a narration of what will be seen, whole and in plain words; the idea in one sentence with **the true thing** it rests on and the recognition it relies on; the layer's own fields; **against the prior read** (raises, fixes, keeps); risk and move. A candidate that cannot be narrated is not yet a candidate.
4. **Engineer before judging:** run the layer's review on each and repair: plain against the bar or the user's intensity → add richness through organization; noisy → simplify or regroup; a part needing what the maker cannot deliver → rebuild around what can be shown.

## 4. Judge and choose
1. **Eliminate** any candidate that contradicts a quality, a mandatory item, the conflict priority, the spine, or a constant of the layer above. The layer's **gates** eliminate regardless of conceptual strength. If every candidate fails a gate, rebuild once from another seed, then take the spine candidate at its best craft.
2. **Compare with the prior read:** the leader must beat it on a named, visible dimension. A candidate that differs only in adjectives, in an idea the finished work would not show, or that a stranger could guess from the category and place, is the prior: rebuild once from another seed, or take the spine candidate.
3. **Rank the survivors** where they differ: **Appeal** (would this audience stop, smile, want it?), **Ownable** (with the name removed, does it still point to this business, and does it fail the swap test?), **Memorable** (recallable in one sentence), **Coherent** (every part follows from the idea and the layer above), **Truthful** (every claim, symbol, and depiction true and respectful), plus the layer's added criterion.
4. **Challenge once:** name the runner-up (a candidate you would ship, never a weak foil) and the strongest case against the leader. The margin is one **decisive criterion** naming an observable property of the finished work, not of the concept or of a business fact. If none can be stated, the candidates tie: take the simpler and record the tie.
5. **Engineer:** each material weakness gets a named move or an accepted cost; each key strength a place where it is amplified. Select, transplant a strength, repair, or combine as a **hybrid** (statable as one idea); a compromise needing "and also" is rejected.
6. **Record:** the choice, its idea and ownable traits, runner-up, decisive criterion, what was kept from the others, obligations, and the layer's **handoff**: what the next layer must keep (constants), may vary (flex), and must achieve.

## 5. Cards (guided mode)
Show only the choice, its reason, and the alternatives, in the user's language, skimmable in seconds, with no internal terms for a new user. Two or three rows, the chosen one first; matters already fixed by the user go in one line "Already settled: …". The layer file gives the template and its step number.
Feedback: acknowledge in one line, rerun what it touches, show only changed rows as Before | After, ask once; after two rounds without agreement choose the best resolution, say so, continue. A question is answered in one or two plain sentences, then the confirmation line repeats. Feedback contradicting a stated quality or fact: say so kindly and offer the closest option keeping both. Feedback that changes the layer above (the business, the audience, the message): re-enter Phase 2 for what it touches; every layer below goes stale. In auto mode no cards appear; the same judging runs silently.

## Gate (every layer; the layer file adds its own)
Every open decision shows its prior read, runner-up, and decisive criterion · candidates were narrated whole, reviewed, and differ on a structural axis · the leader passed the gates and beats the prior read on a named visible dimension · every weakness has a move or an accepted cost · the idea fits one sentence · the handoff is recorded.
