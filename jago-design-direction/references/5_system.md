# Phase 5: the picture as a system

**Needs:** the theme record; the brand record; S; the interpretation. **Produces:** the **direction record**: the chosen picture developed into art direction at ceiling, composition architecture, hero, the treatment of every element, lettering, color logic, supporting graphics, brand presence, ownable traits, guards, and how each tension is carried.

## 1. The chain: inherit, then derive
A distinctive direction is one logic seen everywhere. This phase inherits the brand's constants and the theme's concept and carriers as fixed, and decides how they apply to this design's elements. The parts are developed in this order, each stating in one clause its **parent** (the theme carrier, brand constant, or mandate it follows) and its **reason**, and each reading the parts before it. A part with no parent or reason is a default and is replaced.
**theme's picture → art direction → composition architecture → hero and imagery → lettering → color logic → supporting graphics → information presentation → brand presence → ownable traits → guards**
The parts before *information presentation* set the system's vocabulary (voices, palette roles, shape grammar, imagery); information presentation is the integrating pass that applies that vocabulary to every group and element.
The forces in §2 set the language; the picture is realized through the language, never the reverse. Where they conflict, re-express the picture before bending the language; where the conflict comes from the theme or the brand, repair it there (hierarchy rules, `SKILL.md`). The art direction governs the rest: color follows the rendering mode, lettering shares the carrier's construction logic, composition follows how the lineage treats space and depth. Test the chain once at the end: pick any part and predict the next from the picture and the parts before it; if the prediction fails, the part does not belong.

## 2. The parts
- **Art direction** (the most consequential decision for any image-led piece; derived, never merely named; start from the theme's Look and the brand's lineage, then test and complete):
  1. *Carrier:* what the eye reads first (a character, a photograph, a pattern, the lettering). Its language is built first; everything else conforms to it.
  2. *Forces:* the brand's language and the theme's concept; the audience's visual world and the appeal bar; the business's tier and promise; each mood quality turned into form consequences at the user's intensity (shape, proportion, rhythm, contrast, saturation, line behavior); the carrier's job; the reproduction and what the maker renders dependably.
  3. *Lineage:* the brand's lineage leads, extended to the image-making class this design needs (what the brand's form logic becomes when it draws a hero, models light, builds a scene), as narrow as era, region, and technique allow. State what is adopted from its logic (how it builds form, models light, handles edges, uses color and space, ranks detail) and what is left behind. Adopt its **richness logic** together with its simplifications: a lineage is chosen for the appeal of the finished work in it, never for ease of rendering, and "simple" is an outcome of that logic, never a goal. A second lineage may govern one axis under a stated rule; a pile of unrelated looks fails. A medium or format word (vector, 3D, photographic, flat) is a production class, not a lineage. Never a living artist, studio, or protected character.
  4. *Ceiling axes:* form construction (geometry, proportion, silhouette); line (presence, weight behavior, terminals, color); **color and rendering** (how deeply forms are modelled: flat, tonal, or fully modelled; how light falls and how soft its shading is; highlight and shadow logic; edge treatment; saturation and temperature relations; the **value key**); surface (graphic marks only; a finish is named only where it is how a depicted thing looks, never as a metaphor for the concept); light; detail by rank (the carrier fullest, the rest stepping down). State every axis at the finish level of the appeal bar, in the richest register the audience's world and the maker's strength allow. Choose the plain option on an axis only for a named failure it avoids, or because the lineage's own logic is plain there.
  5. *Tests:* **Strike** (remove medium and format words and bare mood adjectives; a drawable look remains) · **Kinship** (two skilled makers given the paragraph produce recognizably related work) · **Coherence** (every axis follows from the lineage; none cancels another) · **Home turf** (it sits in the visual world the audience already finds appealing) · **Mood** (each quality has form-level carriers on the dominant masses) · **Tool** (the maker renders it dependably; a concretely described rich look usually renders well, so complexity alone never justifies the plainer choice) · **Ceiling** (every axis is at or above the bar; a viewer would rank the result with the best work in this world, not the average).
- **Composition architecture** (the decision that most separates professional from amateur work; stated as zones and relations in plain words, never as numbers):
  - *Focal structure:* the first read, the contrast that makes it decisive, and the second read.
  - *Scale ladder:* how group sizes step by tier.
  - *Interlock:* how the hero and the headline lockup belong to one composition, and how the hero leads the eye into the offer (its gesture, gaze, or direction; overlap, tucking behind, or a shared contour, whichever the lineage suits). Stacked blocks are the failure. The mark is the exception: it stands apart (brand presence).
  - *Depth:* what sits in front and behind, and why.
  - *Zones:* where each group lives relative to the hero and to the others, and what stays clear.
  - *Balance:* the stance and its counterweight; where calm sits so that richness reads as richness.
  - *Route:* the eye's path from first read to action, led by natural cues, not drawn lines.
  - *Edges:* nothing the viewer needs near the trim.
  Where format and load allow one sensible arrangement, state it; where several work equally, state the logic and leave the rest to the maker.
- **Hero and imagery:** what the hero truly is, down to its specific features; the depiction mode follows what must be true: where the viewer must see exactly what is offered, a mode that stylizes it away is ineligible. A hero is chosen only when one subject deserves primary attention and can be depicted truthfully; otherwise there is none, or a small equal set. Inherit the brand's imagery stance and give the hero a **situation** that expresses the theme and the user's qualities and does a job in the composition (what it points at, looks at, holds, leans toward). A figure (mascot, character) is decided at identity level: kind, character, three to five distinctive features that read at distance, proportions, consistency across images; its simplification and rendering are the art direction's output. Features the maker knows from the subject's kind are left to it. A supplied figure is never redesigned. Depict the specific thing, never a generic glyph interchangeable between subjects.
- **Lettering:** the headline, name, and prices are **designed objects**, not type set beside a picture. They inherit the brand's lettering voice and are built in the logic of the art direction: construction (weight, width, terminals, rhythm), and how each sits in the picture (outline, depth, tilt, color relation to the hero) at the finish level of the bar. Roles that must differ (display, informational, numerals, small print) differ by more than size. Items of one role share one treatment whatever their length. Letters are never compressed, stretched, or slanted to fit. No font names unless a brand requires one.
- **Color logic:** state what color does here (organize, signal, separate, evoke). It inherits the brand's color identity; decided here are the roles and rough proportions (a dominant field, a supporting field, an accent, an ink for text), harmony, temperature, and the **value key**: the light or dark balance by area, ground included, which must support every quality. Depth comes from tints and shade relationships derived from the palette, never from leaving it. The strongest chroma goes to the first read; area is not prominence. Colors the user named or a supplied identity fixes are fixed; a derived brand color is named by hue, role, and intensity; text color is chosen for legibility; hex only for supplied brand colors.
- **Supporting graphics:** every element has a job (function, what is lost without it, effect); two with one function merge. Order of enrichment: deepen the hero and lettering, then interlock the major elements, then atmosphere that belongs to the world, then small supporters. **Organization, not count, limits richness:** every addition shares the brand's form logic and the theme's carriers, ranks below the first read, and stays out of the zones of exact text. A stated quality of energy, abundance, richness, or warmth sets a **minimum**: the first read and the ground both carry it at that intensity, and plainness against such a quality fails. An element present only because pieces of this kind have it is replaced by something specific to this business or removed; the audience's own signifiers (appeal bar, *meet*) are not defaults.
- **Information presentation (every group and element is derived, not styled by habit).** For each group and each visual element, decide in this order and record one line:
  1. **Job:** its communication purpose: what the viewer understands or does because of it (from the groups and tiers).
  2. **Rank:** its importance and relation to the others: its tier, what it leads, what it follows, what it must stay beside (items that label, price, or explain a depicted thing belong with it).
  3. **Theme expression:** how the concept shows in it (which carrier), so it belongs to the theme's world instead of sitting on it.
  4. **Place:** its zone, depth, position on the route, and interlock.
  5. **Treatment:** the artistic and structural form its lettering, grouping device, and rendering take, drawn from the lettering voice, color roles, shape logic, and imagery set above, so no element needs a new invention.
  Treatments differ by role inside one grammar: equal items share one design; items of different roles differ by more than size; no element takes a treatment because its neighbor has one. **Grouping devices:** the lightest first (spacing and alignment, then a rule or tonal step, then a container); a container needs a reason the lighter ones fail. Any device that exists is built from the brand's shape logic and the theme's structure, never a default rectangle or pill, and containers holding different roles differ at a glance.
- **Brand presence:**
  - *The mark:* a supplied logo is exact; a new mark follows the brand record's narration, construction, and lockup, its letters complete and in order. It stands as a distinct object with its own clear field, never overlapped by the hero, lettering, or supporters, never fused with them or restyled. Its tier follows its job in the interpretation: glance where recognition drives the action, otherwise a close-tier signature.
  - *The language:* visible with the mark covered: constants honored, the flex used as the theme directs, the signatures deployed within their scopes.
- **Ownable traits (one to three):** chosen, not invented: from the brand's signatures and the theme's devices, the ones that recur here and make the piece recognizable, never the category's stock devices. Each is drawable and dependable for the maker (no exact paths or counts) and states its scope: where it appears and where it never does (never across mandatory text or the first read unless that is the trait). A trait applied everywhere is a filter. More than three dilute identity.
- **Guards:** failures the user would also call failures, taken from the prior read's Fix list, each stated as its positive opposite. A guard that names an unwanted object invites it: name an object only when the maker commits it unprompted (extra text, watermarks, invented marks). At most three guards beyond the user's exclusions and the text rule. Never a guard that removes what the user's own words welcome, or that blocks richness the quality requires.
- **Tensions:** confirm each lead side against the assembled direction and record what the other side keeps through structure.


## 3. Direction check (once)
Run the picture review (`4_theme.md` §5) on the assembled direction. Then:
- **Ancestry:** does every part name a parent in the theme, the brand, or a mandate, and does the chain predict itself?
- **Cover test:** cover the mark: is it still this brand? Cover the text: does it still do this design's job?
- **Family test:** pick any two elements: do they read as one hand, treated by role and not alike?
- Does the picture beat the prior read on a named visible dimension, and does every part serve a named item of keep, raise, or fix? Does every user quality have two carriers on the dominant masses at its intensity, and does any decision signal its opposite? Does anything read as a literal material or object where a graphic form was meant? Are the ownable traits present in the hero, the lettering, and the supporting system, within their scopes? Does anything fixed here belong to the maker (§4)?
A conflict returns to the responsible part, or to the layer that caused it; two cycles at most.

## 4. What the direction leaves to the maker
Exact placement, sizes, spacing, type pairing within the voice, the exact position of lights, the position and count of small decorative elements, finish detail below the ownable traits. The direction fixes intent, relations, and defining traits, never a specification.

## Gate (fails if any is false)
The chain holds and every part names its parent and reason · the art direction passes its seven tests · the composition architecture covers focal structure, scale, interlock, depth, zones, balance, and route · the hero has a situation and depicts only what is true · every group and element has job, rank, theme expression, place, and treatment · the mark stands apart and whole · every picture mandate is answered by id, every quality and tension is carried at its intensity · the picture review and the direction check passed · nothing is fixed that belongs to the maker. Then append the record.
