# Phase 3b: the direction as a system

**Needs:** the chosen idea and its record; S; the Phase 2 record. **Produces:** the **direction record**: idea, signature moves, visual language, hero, color logic, lettering voice, hierarchy and grouping relations, supporting-visual stance, identity decisions, boundaries, point of tension, and how each tension is carried.

## 1. The chain
A distinctive direction is one logic seen everywhere. Develop the parts in this order; each states in one clause **because of what in the idea** it is so, and each reads the ones before it. A part that cannot name its reason is a default and is replaced.
**idea → signature moves → visual language → hero and imagery → color logic → lettering voice → hierarchy and grouping → supporting visuals → identity → boundaries**
Test the chain once at the end: pick any part and predict the next from the idea and the parts before it; if the prediction fails, the part does not belong. The direction states what it fixes; the maker decides what it does not (§4).

## 2. The parts
- **Signature moves (one to three):** the recurring devices that make the piece recognizable and ownable, each applied wherever it can be, not once. They are derived from the idea and the true specifics, not from the category's stock devices. Everything else supports them and stays quieter. More than three dilute the identity.
- **Visual language:** a single synthesis in specialist terms. *Lineage and borrowed traits* (never a living artist, studio, or protected character; a lineage is used with its logic understood, not as a surface); *form logic* (geometry, corner and edge behavior, how shapes are built); *line* (weight, variation, terminals); *surface and finish* (material impression, grain, how form is rendered and where rendering stops); *light* (direction, quality, key); *detail steps by rank* (the hero fullest, the rest stepping down). Test: strike category and medium words (flat, vector, sticker, cartoon, pop) and bare mood adjectives (cute, modern, clean); what remains must still describe a drawable look. Renderability is judged only on what must be exact (text, counted forms, mark geometry), never as a reason to choose the plainest look: a concretely described rich language usually renders well.
- **Hero and imagery:** what the hero truly is, down to its specific features; the depiction mode (photographic, illustrated, constructed, typographic, abstract) follows what must be true: where the viewer must see exactly what is offered, a mode that stylizes it away is ineligible. A hero is chosen only when one subject deserves primary attention and can be depicted truthfully; otherwise there is none, or a small equal set. A figure (mascot, character) is decided at identity level: kind, character, three to five distinctive features, proportions, consistency across images; a supplied figure is never redesigned, and a real likeness is never used. Depict the specific thing, never a generic glyph interchangeable between subjects.
- **Color logic:** state what color does here (organize, signal, separate, evoke, depart), then roles and rough proportions (a calm dominant field, a supporting field, a sparing accent, an ink for text), harmony, temperature, and the **value key**: the light or dark balance by area, ground included. Dark masses make a low-key piece however light the accents, so the key must support every quality. The strongest chroma goes to the first read; area is not prominence. Colors the user named are fixed; text color is chosen for legibility; hex only for supplied brand colors.
- **Lettering voice:** what the lettering says about this subject, drawn from the lettering traditions in the pool, never the category's stock face. Roles that must differ (display, informational, numerals, small print) differ by more than size: construction, weight, width, posture, case, spacing. Items of one role share one treatment whatever their length. Letters are never compressed, stretched, or slanted to fit. No font names unless a brand requires one.
- **Hierarchy and grouping** (the one composition decision made beforehand): exactly one first read at glance, then the order; groups formed by job and referent, with what labels, prices, or explains a depicted thing kept with it; what belongs with the hero; where quiet stays open and what it separates; the balance stance and its counterweight; the reading route. Relations only, never placement. When content is dense, relief comes from structure and grouping, never from ornament.
- **Supporting visuals:** every element carries a purpose (its function, what is lost without it, its effect); two with one function merge. Enrich before adding: deepen the hero and lettering, then link major elements, then decided atmosphere, then small supporters. Detect defaults: an element present only because pieces of this kind have it is replaced by a specific one or removed; ornament that merely restates the category is removed; glossy gradients, generic soft 3D, confetti, and stock icon sets need a reason rooted in the idea. Count resources by kind, never by theme: dense content allows up to 2 supporter families (pattern or texture, atmosphere effects, edge ornament, dividers, spot illustration), 3 container designs, 3 rendering-treatment kinds (outline, shadow, gradient, texture, highlight, dimension); moderate 4, 4, 3; light 6, 5, 4. Exceed only by naming the deficit that earns it and the family dropped to pay for it. Plainness is a decision, not a default; it holds when the signature moves keep the piece specific.
- **Containers and grouping devices:** the lightest device first (spacing and alignment, then a rule or tonal step, then a container); a container needs a reason the lighter ones fail. One shape language (corner and edge geometry, material impression) shared by all containers, rules, and supporters; containers holding different roles differ at a glance; equal items share one design. The default form of the category is the baseline: it wins only by naming the criterion the alternatives failed.
- **Identity:** a supplied logo or wordmark is exact. A new one is decided as an **idea**: an ownable, grounded device tying the name to the business's truth (never a styled name alone), weighed against one alternative; its construction is left to the maker, its letters stay complete and in order, and it is distinct from the surrounding lettering by form, color role, scale, or isolation. If the hero or idea already fixes the form, only the device is open.
- **Boundaries:** what the piece must never become, taken from the baseline, the neighbors' look, and common defaults; each later stated as a positive opposite.
- **Point of tension:** at most one deliberate break from the piece's own system, only where it lowers reading effort or raises delight; none when the idea already supplies the unexpected or the qualities call for calm.
- **Tensions:** confirm each lead side against the assembled direction and record what the other side keeps.

## 3. Direction check (once)
Do the parts reinforce one another, and do accepted costs compound? Is the chain unbroken? Does the direction answer the weakest link in the real setting, hold with the name removed, and differ from the baseline on the three named dimensions? Does every quality have a visible carrier and does any decision signal its opposite? Can the content hierarchy be carried? Is there exactly one first read? Are the signature moves present in the hero, the lettering, and the supporting system, not in one place only? Does anything fixed here belong to the maker (§4)? A conflict returns to the responsible part; two cycles at most.

## 4. What the direction leaves to the maker
Placement and composition detail, sizes, spacing, type pairing within the voice, lighting setup, the position and count of decorative or supporting elements, finish detail below the signature moves. The direction fixes intent and defining traits, never a specification.

## Gate (fails if any is false)
Every part names its reason and the chain holds · one to three signature moves recur across the system · the language passes the strike test and the hero depicts only what is true · the value key supports every quality · exactly one first read · supporting resources are within the caps, counted by kind, with no default fill · every quality is carried and every tension carried · the direction differs from the baseline on three named dimensions · nothing is fixed that belongs to the maker. Then append the record.
