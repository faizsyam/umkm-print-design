# Exploration protocol (Phases 2 to 5)

Which decisions are explored and how much, the direction protocol, the compact comparison, and the specialist terms that name things precisely.

**Inputs needed:** The decision being made, its phase, and the openness map in S.

Read once on entering Phase 2. Phase files point here.

## 1. Decision classes

Exploration is spent where a wrong choice is costly and the brief leaves the choice open. Everything else is inherited, derived, or specified directly.

| Class | Decisions | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Direction | Core idea, style, hero (Phase 3); a new logo mark and lockup, a created lead figure (Phase 4, with the extended development in `06_identity_unit_and_copy_treatment.md` C); spatial strategy (Phase 5) | Section 2, sized by openness |
| System | Phase 4: palette and value key, type roles, shape and container language, supporter policy, ground, derived from the Direction choices as one decision | Section 3, one comparison for the whole system |
| Execution | Copy treatments, line breaks, wording of non-mandatory text, small item art, icons, edge and surface details, decorative placement | Specified directly in the Phase 6 prompt: no log item, no comparison, no purpose line |
| Escalation | Any System or Execution choice with a named risk: it conflicts with a quality requirement or fixed asset, exact text is at renderability risk, it contradicts an earlier decision, or the user doubted it | Section 3, for that choice only |

Phase 2's differentiation, weakest link, and departure take a compact comparison when the brief leaves them open and are decided directly otherwise. Phases 1 and 6 hold no exploration.

- **Openness.** For each Direction decision the brief is read as **Open** (only function and subject given), **Steered** (some attributes fixed: hero subject, palette, mood, a named style, a stated placement), or **Fixed** (the user, a supplied asset, or evidence decides). Fixed: record as fixed (`prompt_and_reply_audit.md` section 11), no cards. Steered: the protocol runs on the open remainder only, with at least two candidates. Open: the protocol with at least three. A rich brief shortens the work and a blank one deepens it. Content volume, crowded surroundings, and several deliverables never raise openness; they raise the load and fit work.
- **Inheritance.** A decision an earlier decision fixes (a corner geometry, a type role, a palette role, the treatment vocabulary) is applied, not compared. Decisions that read each other and apply to the same group are one decision with one decisive criterion.
- **Stop rule.** A decision is closed when its decisive criterion is stated and no recorded check fails it. It reopens only by naming a failed check (`workflow_and_state.md` section 4). Loops cap at two cycles.

## 2. Direction protocol

1. **Name the apparent option** as the benchmark and state the pressure the decision resolves (a requirement, a tension, the weakest link). Derive candidates from that pressure, not from the category.
2. **Write the candidates** (counts by openness). At most one is apparent and at least one comes from beyond it; they differ on a structural axis and are sourced differently (product truth, audience moment, environment, differentiator, name or origin, material, Visual source inventory, territory survey). Fill the phase's card fields in specialist terms (section 4) and finish all before judging. A card whose depiction mode is ineligible (`03_core_idea_style_and_hero.md` E) or that fails a hard constraint (a spatial strategy failing the fit screen, `11_space_plan_and_spatial_strategy.md` C) is dropped after one line.
3. **Judge**, last-written first. Eliminate any card that contradicts a quality requirement or mandatory content (unless `global_standards.md` section 7 forces the trade). Rate survivors in one line each, only where they differ, on the lenses that discriminate: quality requirements; environment (neighbors, light, distance, departure and fit); capacity and production (load fit, material, renderability, `terms_brief_and_context.md`); coherence with decisions made; brand, audience, culture. An argument from one lens is not an evaluation.
4. **Challenge once.** Name the runner-up, the strongest case against the leader and what must hold for it, and the leader's margin as one decisive criterion naming an observable property. If none can be stated after step 5's rebuild, the candidates are equivalent: take the one with the lowest space and rendering cost and record the tie. An apparent option wins only by naming the criterion on which the others fell short.
5. **Test the leader.** *Predictable?* A stranger could guess it from category and place: rebuild once and retest; a second failure takes the best available resolution and is recorded. (A convention the audience relies on to read or use the piece stays where the medium reading says departing costs them.) *Specific?* Nameable by lineage and defining traits, derived from this brief, failing unchanged for a competitor. *Ambitious?* A merely competent candidate beats an ambitious one only on a stated criterion.
6. **Engineer.** Each material weakness gets a named design move or a recorded accepted cost; each key strength a place where it is amplified. Select outright, transplant a strength, repair a weakness, or combine under one logic (hybrid, never compromise).
7. **Record** in the Design Log: Specification, Source, Basis, and Rejected (apparent option, runner-up, decisive criterion, what was kept); each mitigation, accepted cost, and amplify-here move as a carry-forward obligation. A missing field means the decision is not made.

## 3. Compact comparison

1. Name the apparent option.
2. Compare two or more alternatives (three where real options exist) that differ in logic, not detail, one line each against quality compatibility, environment, and coherence.
3. Choose; state the decisive criterion and the weakness handled in a line.

Where evidence leaves one defensible option, say so and manufacture no alternatives. The System decision compares two whole-system variants only where the direction leaves a real fork; otherwise it is derived and recorded in one line per part.

## 4. Specialist terminology

Name things at a specialist's precision, and use a term only if it can be defined. An undefinable prestigious word is vagueness and is cut.
- **Style:** lineage and defining traits.
- **Imagery:** depiction mode, lineage, proportions, linework, technique, texture, detail level, color treatment, stylization, light, finish.
- **Color:** hue family, value structure, chroma, temperature, harmony structure, area proportion.
- **Type:** voice and lineage, classification, stroke contrast, proportion, x-height, widths, spacing and density, alignment, orientation.
- **Composition:** axis, alignment, grid logic, figure-ground, negative-space shape, interlock and layering, focal hierarchy, grouping, visual weight, tension.
- **Decoration:** motif family, repeat logic, scale, density, contrast, placement.
- **Reproduction:** ink, substrate, finish, display.

## Quality checks

- Each Direction decision is recorded at its openness: apparent option, candidates, runner-up, decisive criterion, and the three tests; each material weakness has a move or an accepted cost.
- No System or Execution choice was explored beyond its class without a named risk.
